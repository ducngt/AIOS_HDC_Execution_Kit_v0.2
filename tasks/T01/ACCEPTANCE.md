# T01 Acceptance Criteria

T01 may be accepted only when all of the following are demonstrated.

1. The Canonical Meta-Model is machine-verifiable.
2. Canonical object identity, version, and reference rules are explicit.
3. Identity, Role, and Authority are structurally distinct.
4. Capability and Authority cannot be represented as the same concept.
5. Domain definitions do not hard-code Organization structure.
6. Canonical references use a consistent machine-verifiable form.
7. Current state, lifecycle, history, and provenance are structurally distinct.
8. Invalid canonical objects fail validation deterministically.
9. Cross-object references can be validated.
10. The meta-model has an explicit versioning strategy.
11. T00 HDC Guard remains valid and passing.
12. Verification can be replayed from a clean checkout.
13. Engineering Evidence is produced and linked to the T01 Change Package.
14. Human Acceptance remains an explicit authority boundary.

Acceptance of T01 must be performed by a Human and recorded separately
from AI-generated engineering evidence.
